# Onboarding: what competitors do and what we do better

Research from 13 October 2026 (help pages, reviews and teardown sites; full screen-by-screen flows of several apps sit behind sign-up, so some question orders are partial).

## What Track Me Fit does

- **7 short steps, no paywall, no sales calls.** A visible progress bar, and "Skip setup and start logging" on the first screen for people who just want to log (FitNotes style). A skip is remembered.
- **About you:** name, sex, date of birth, height, weight, and an optional waist size. BMI shows live with **Indian cut-offs** (normal 18.5 to 22.9) and the healthy weight range for the height; waist is flagged at 90 cm for men and 80 cm for women.
- **Health check as one checklist:** diabetes, blood pressure or heart, thyroid, PCOS, kidney disease, pregnancy or breastfeeding, weight-loss medicine (GLP-1), eating disorder, injury, plus free text. Each tick shows a doctor note and changes the plan: pregnancy means no weight-loss targets; eating disorder, diabetes or kidney disease allow only the gentlest pace; kidney disease caps protein.
- **Training:** daily activity, workouts a week, lifting experience, and where you train (gym, home with dumbbells, no equipment).
- **Goal with a pace and a goal date:** pace in % of body weight a week (steady 0.5 % recommended for fat loss), an optional target weight checked against the goal, and a live "you'd reach 58 kg by February 2027".
- **Indian food habits:** veg, eggetarian, non-veg, vegan or Jain; no onion or garlic; fasting (weekly, Ekadashi, Navratri, Ramadan); allergies.
- **A typical day instead of a calorie guess:** tap counts of roti, rice, dal, sabzi, curd, milk, chai, eggs, meat, fruit, snacks and sweets. Values come from our own food database. Shows calories and protein per kg, and flags low protein.
- **Your plan at the end:** BMI, maintenance as a range ("a formula's guess; accurate after 2 to 3 weeks of logging"), how the typical day compares, the goal date, and **suggested** targets with every reason listed. The person taps "Use these targets" or "I'll set my own" (coach or own numbers); nothing is applied without their tap.
