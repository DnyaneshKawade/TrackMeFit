# Open Food Facts: Indian packaged products

`india_products.csv`: 3,547 Indian packaged products with barcode, name, brand, pack size and label calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat (per 100 g, or per 100 ml where `per` says so). Made with `scripts/off_extract_india.py` from the Open Food Facts India export of 12 Oct 2026 (25,389 products; most lacked full nutrition values).
