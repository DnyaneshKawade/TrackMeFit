# USDA SR Legacy macros

`sr_legacy_macros.csv`: calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat per 100 g for all 7,793 foods in USDA SR Legacy, made with `scripts/usda/extract_macros.py` from `FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_csv_2018-04.zip`.

Public domain (CC0). Citation: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central, 2019. fdc.nal.usda.gov.

Carbohydrate is USDA's "carbohydrate, by difference", which includes fibre.
